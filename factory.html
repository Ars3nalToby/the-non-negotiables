      How the factory works

      35 production runs, one per season

      The line

      Each season runs once. Departures walk off the pitch and ride the despatch belt out first. Then the new signings come in on the intake belt, get stamped at the press and walk to their spot. Honours drop into the cabinet. The Invincibles' one is gold, because of course it is.

      Who counts

      First-team regulars and the signings people remember, not every academy kid who got one League Cup run-out. A player out on loan for a whole season counts as gone that season, which is why Tierney and Elneny turn up twice. Henry's 2012 loan counts. Obviously.

      Getting around

      Drag to take the camera, then scroll to zoom (on a phone: swipe sideways, then pinch). Intake, Squad and Despatch jump to each end of the floor. Click any shirt for the player's record. Share sends a link to the season on screen, so you can settle the argument about 2007/08 properly.

      Where the data comes from

      Squads, managers, captains, finishes and honours compiled by hand from the public record, with the 2025/26 and 2026/27 windows checked against contemporary reporting. Who makes each season's squad is this site's own selection.

      The 3D bit

      Built with three.js (MIT licence), served from this site rather than a third-party CDN. Nothing is fetched from anywhere else, and apart from the site-wide visit counter, nothing you do in the factory is recorded.

Special edition

Martin Ødegaard · the captain

The Captain

In May he became the first Arsenal captain since Patrick Vieira to lift the league trophy. In November he told Norwegian reporters that talks on a new contract haven't started. This is about both: why he is one of the very best players to wear the armband here, and why his next contract is the most interesting one the club has left to do.

Updated 18 November 2026 · Arsenal have said nothing about his contract. When they do, this page changes.

The armband

Since July 2022

Arteta gave it to him on 30 July 2022. More than four years on, nobody has held the job as long since Tony Adams.

The trophy

Lifted

Selhurst Park, 24 May 2026, after a 2-1 win over Palace on the final day. Only the third Arsenal captain of the Premier League era to do it, after Adams and Vieira.

The contract

Talks not started

His deal runs to June 2028, the same as Rice's. His own words this month: happy, wants to stay a long time, but "we haven't started on that yet".

How he got here

  1. 2014Fifteen, and already scoring for Strømsgodset

    Became the youngest goalscorer in the history of Norway's top division, and made his senior Norway debut the same year, also at fifteen.

  2. January 2015Real Madrid, and all that comes with it

    The most talked-about teenager in Europe chose Madrid and became the youngest player in their history. Then came the part people forget: years of Castilla, a few minutes here and there, and a growing sense that being a wonderkid is not a career.

  3. 2017 – 2020Heerenveen, Vitesse, Real Sociedad

    Three loans, three steps. The Dutch years taught him to play in a team that needed him. San Sebastián is where he looked like a La Liga player rather than a project.

  4. January 2021On loan at Arsenal

    Signed for six months to fix a creative hole Arteta's side had been stuck with all season. By the summer the club wanted to keep him.

  5. August 2021Permanent, for around £30m

    An initial £30m to Real Madrid. Measured against what he became, one of the best bits of business the club has done this century.

  6. 30 July 2022Captain

    Arteta gave him the armband after a season in which he had quietly become the person everyone looked to. He was 23.

  7. 2022/23Fifteen league goals, and the first title charge

    Joint top scorer with Saka and Martinelli, PFA Team of the Year, Player of the Season. The title went to City at the end, but the team, and the captain, had arrived.

  8. 2023/24Player of the Season again

    Back-to-back, a year before Rice started doing the same. Another title race, lost to City by two points.

  9. 2024/25The ankle

    Ligament damage on Norway duty kept him out for a long spell in the autumn, and the season never quite became his.

  10. 24 May 2026The trophy, at Selhurst Park

    An injury-disrupted season of shoulder and knee problems, a quieter one for goals and assists, and it ended with him lifting the trophy in front of the away end, twenty-two years after Vieira. The quiet seasons count too, when this is how they finish.

  11. Summer 2026Norway's captain at a World Cup

    Norway reached the quarter-finals, where England beat them 2-1. Small consolation that it was Rice and co.

  12. November 2026Hull, Copenhagen, and the contract question

    Scored the winner against Hull before the break, then had a hand in two of Norway's three goals in Copenhagen, forcing the keeper's error for the first and scoring the late equaliser himself, to take them into the Nations League quarter-finals. In between, he told reporters that contract talks haven't begun.

Why he is one of the very best

01He is the system, not just a part of it

The usual way to judge a number 8 or 10 is goals and assists, and on that measure Ødegaard's best seasons are very good rather than outrageous. The reason Arteta built the team around him is the work that doesn't show up there. Arsenal press as a unit, and the press needs someone to start it: the player who reads when the opponent's pass is coming and steps up, so the other nine know to go with him. For most of his time here that has been Ødegaard. His other job is setting the rhythm: when to slow it down and keep the ball, when to play the forward pass that changes the speed. You notice it when he's missing more than when he plays, which is the best compliment you can pay that kind of player.

That is also why the Vieira comparison works better than it looks. They are nothing alike as players: Vieira won games by running through people. What they have in common is that each was the player the whole team was shaped around, and each made the side's standards their own. A captain of a team that wins the league has to be that, not just the player with the armband.

Adams, Vieira, Ødegaard. That is the whole list, and none of them got there by accident.

02The case against, honestly

Two things make his next contract harder than Rice's. The first is injuries: the ankle in 2024/25, then shoulder and knee problems in the title season, then another ankle scare with Norway this September. The second is that the title-winning season was his quietest for output, while others carried more of the attacking load. Neither makes him less important. Both are the things a club weighs when it is deciding how long a contract a 28-year-old creative midfielder should get.

03How this one is likely to play out

Contract leverage runs on time. With eighteen months left, Arsenal hold most of it. Wait another six months and the balance shifts: a player with a year left can be sold for a fee or kept and risked on a free, and every month after that is worth less to the club. That is why clubs that want to keep someone usually try to sort it before the last year starts, and why Rice was done first.

Our read, labelled as one: the club offers a new deal in 2027, shorter than Rice's (three years plus an option rather than five) and built around availability as well as status. He has said he wants to stay, which means a deal of that kind is likely to work. The real warning sign would be silence into next summer. If it is still unsigned at the start of 2027/28, the Bayern stories stop being rumours and start being a plan.

What would change this page

Talks begin

A credible report that his representatives and the club have actually met would move the contract box from "not started" to "in talks".

The length

Whatever he signs, the length tells you how the club rates the next five years against the last four. That number matters more than the wage.

The summer

Still no deal by the summer of 2027 would mean a year left, and a very different conversation. We'd say so here.

# Shotlist — "The 4am Kick-off"

Five shots, one continuous mood: a supporter waking up in the dark on
the other side of the world, cut against the match itself already
under way in the light. Loops seamlessly (shot 5 should read as able
to precede shot 1 again without a hard seam — match the wide dark
gradient of shot 1's opening frame to shot 5's closing frame if you
can).

**Hard constraints for every shot** — no exceptions: no Arsenal crest,
cannon, wordmark, kit, sponsor logos, or any other club marks. No
identifiable players, staff, or real people (silhouettes/soft-focus
crowd only, unbranded). No readable text anywhere in frame (no shirt
names, no signage, no scoreboards). Stadium shots generic and
unidentifiable — no specific ground's architecture. Anamorphic
2.39:1 framing, shallow depth of field, slow push-ins or a slow dolly
only — no handheld, no whip pans, no fast cuts. Practical light
sources only (lamps, phone glow, kettle steam backlit, floodlight
haze). Deep navy shadows, red/amber accent light, halation around
bright sources, visible film grain.

---

## Shot 1 — The dark room

- **Duration:** 6–7s
- **Shot size:** Medium close, a phone screen's glow lighting a face
  from below/side — face can be out of focus or turned away, this is
  about the light, not a portrait.
- **Lens feel:** 50mm-equivalent, shallow DOF (f/1.4–f/2 look),
  slight anamorphic oval bokeh on the phone glow.
- **Camera move:** Static or the slowest possible push-in (barely
  perceptible over the shot's length).
- **Lighting:** Near-total darkness except the cold blue-white phone
  glow. A clock or phone display reads 4:30 but keep any digits soft/
  slightly defocused so it isn't legible as "text," just a mood cue.
- **Color:** Deep navy/black shadows, cold blue key light, no warm
  light yet — this is the coldest shot in the sequence.
- **AI image-to-video prompt:**
  > Cinematic anamorphic shot, 2.39:1, a dark bedroom before dawn, a
  > phone screen glowing on a nightstand, cold blue light spilling
  > across the ceiling, shallow depth of field, soft bokeh, film
  > grain, halation, slow static hold, no people in focus, no visible
  > text, moody and quiet, blue hour, 35mm anamorphic lens look

## Shot 2 — Kettle and blue hour

- **Duration:** 7–8s
- **Shot size:** Wide-medium, kitchen counter or windowsill in
  foreground, city/river skyline soft in the background through glass.
- **Lens feel:** Wider, ~35mm-equivalent, foreground kettle steam
  sharp-ish, background skyline soft.
- **Camera move:** Slow lateral dolly, or a slow push through the
  steam toward the window.
- **Lighting:** Warm practical lamp or under-cabinet light on the
  kettle; cool blue exterior light through the window — a warm/cool
  split that's the visual hinge of the whole piece.
- **Color:** Warm amber foreground, cool navy blue-hour skyline behind
  — first appearance of the amber accent that carries through the
  rest of the sequence.
- **AI image-to-video prompt:**
  > Cinematic anamorphic shot, 2.39:1, backlit steam rising from a
  > kettle on a kitchen counter at blue hour, warm amber lamplight in
  > the foreground, a soft-focus city skyline and river visible
  > through a window behind, shallow depth of field, slow dolly move,
  > film grain, halation around the light source, no people, no
  > readable text, warm-cool color contrast, anamorphic lens flare

## Shot 3 — The empty pitch

- **Duration:** 6–7s
- **Shot size:** Wide, a rain-soaked football pitch under floodlights,
  no players, no crowd — just the haze and the wet grass reflecting
  light.
- **Lens feel:** Wide-ish, deep-feeling but still shallow enough that
  the far floodlight towers soften.
- **Camera move:** Very slow push-in across the pitch, low-ish angle
  so wet grass reflections read clearly.
- **Lighting:** Hard floodlight beams cutting through visible haze/
  mist, practical-source halation, no daylight.
- **Color:** Cool white-green floodlight wash, deep black sky, minimal
  warm accent — this is the "match cut" beat, deliberately colder and
  stiller than the shots either side of it.
- **AI image-to-video prompt:**
  > Cinematic anamorphic shot, 2.39:1, an empty rain-soaked football
  > pitch at night under floodlights, visible haze in the light beams,
  > wet grass reflecting the floodlights, no players, no crowd, no
  > logos or signage, wide static composition with a very slow push
  > in, film grain, halation, cold color palette, still and quiet,
  > generic unbranded stadium

## Shot 4 — Red smoke, the crowd behind

- **Duration:** 6–7s
- **Shot size:** Medium, floodlight beams with drifting red smoke cutting
  through them, unbranded crowd silhouettes soft and out of focus in
  the far background.
- **Lens feel:** Longer focal length compression (~85mm-equivalent),
  very shallow DOF so the crowd is pure soft-focus shape, never
  identifiable as individuals.
- **Camera move:** Slow push-in through the smoke.
- **Lighting:** Floodlights from behind/side, red smoke lit from
  within, silhouettes only — no faces, no shirt numbers, no colours
  that read as a specific kit.
- **Color:** This is the warmest, most saturated shot — red/amber
  smoke against the cool floodlight haze, first real intensity of the
  sequence.
- **AI image-to-video prompt:**
  > Cinematic anamorphic shot, 2.39:1, red smoke drifting through
  > stadium floodlight beams at night, an out-of-focus silhouette of a
  > crowd in the far background with no identifiable faces or shirts,
  > shallow depth of field, slow push in, film grain, halation, warm
  > red and amber tones against cool white floodlights, no logos, no
  > readable text, generic unbranded stadium atmosphere

## Shot 5 — First light

- **Duration:** 6–7s
- **Shot size:** Back to the bedroom/window from shot 1–2's world —
  medium, a window with first daylight breaking through, same room
  feel as the opener to close the loop.
- **Lens feel:** ~50mm-equivalent, shallow DOF, soft flare off the
  window glass as light hits it.
- **Camera move:** Slowest possible push toward the window, or static.
- **Lighting:** Warm dawn light finally breaking through — the payoff
  of the warm/cool tension built across shots 2–4.
- **Color:** Warm gold/amber dawn light overtaking the navy shadow —
  should feel like resolution, and its final frame should sit close
  enough to shot 1's opening darkness that the loop back to shot 1
  doesn't jar.
- **AI image-to-video prompt:**
  > Cinematic anamorphic shot, 2.39:1, warm golden dawn light breaking
  > through a bedroom window, soft lens flare on the glass, shallow
  > depth of field, slow static push in, film grain, halation, warm
  > amber tones replacing cool blue shadow, quiet and still, no
  > people, no readable text, seamless loop-friendly framing

Not a scoreboard, not a listicle

The best Arsenal information isn't on this site — it's on theirs

Nothing below is copied. Every post is embedded straight from that account's own X feed, using X's own embed code — the same mechanic as the Fan Art page. It shows their real handle, their real avatar, and a follow button, and every click goes to x.com, not here. If an embed doesn't load (ad blockers and privacy browsers often stop it deliberately), the link underneath goes straight to their profile instead.

This isn't a ranking of follower counts. It's a short list of people who reliably say something true, first, or better than the rest of Arsenal Twitter — each one added because their handle was checked by hand, not pulled off a "who to follow" listicle.
